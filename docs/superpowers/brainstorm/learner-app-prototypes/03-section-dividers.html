Use for hero sections or full backgrounds. 7–9 nodes, rich texture.

Design Principles

  • Left-to-right flow — paths always flow in reading direction
  • Never cross — paths never intersect or overlap
  • Cubic beziers — smooth, organic curves, never angular
  • Low opacity — decorative, not distracting (8–15% opacity)
  • Dashed stroke — 8px dash, 5px gap — never solid
