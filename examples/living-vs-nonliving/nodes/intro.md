# Living vs Non-Living Things

Look around you. Some things are **alive** — they grow, eat, and move. Other things are **not alive** — they never grow or need food.

## Living Things

Living things:

- 🌱 **Grow** — A seed grows into a plant
- 🍽️ **Eat** — Animals need food
- 🏃 **Move** — Living things can move by themselves
- 👶 **Reproduce** — They make more of themselves

## Non-Living Things

Non-living things:

- Do **not** grow
- Do **not** need food
- Do **not** move by themselves
- Do **not** reproduce

> A **plant** is a living thing. A **rock** is not a living thing.

> ⚠️ **Don't be fooled!** The Sun gives light and heat, but it is **not** alive. Fire moves and grows, but it is **not** alive either.
