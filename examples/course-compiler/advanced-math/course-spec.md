# Module 1: Algebra Basics

This module covers fundamental algebraic concepts.

Estimated time: 10 hours

## Lesson 1.1: Linear Equations

**Objectives:**

- Solve linear equations with one variable
- Graph linear equations on a coordinate plane

Linear equations form the foundation of algebra. A linear equation is an equation that makes a straight line when graphed.

### Activity: Exercise

Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22

### Quiz: Linear Equations Quiz

1. What is the slope of y = 2x + 3?

- [x] 2
- [ ] 3
- [ ] 1
- [ ] 0

2. Solve for x: 2x - 4 = 10

- [x] 7
- [ ] 6
- [ ] 5
- [ ] 8

## Lesson 1.2: Quadratic Functions

**Objectives:**

- Identify quadratic functions
- Solve quadratic equations using the quadratic formula

Quadratic functions have the form f(x) = ax² + bx + c.

### Activity: Exercise

Use the quadratic formula to solve: x² - 5x + 6 = 0

# Module 2: Geometry

This module covers basic geometric concepts and proofs.

Estimated time: 10 hours
Prerequisites: Module 1 (Algebra Basics)

## Lesson 2.1: Triangles

**Objectives:**

- Classify triangles by sides and angles
- Apply the Pythagorean theorem

Triangles are three-sided polygons with properties that make them fundamental to geometry.

### Activity: Exercise

Calculate the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs of length 3 and 4.

### Quiz: Triangle Quiz

1. What is the sum of angles in any triangle?

- [x] 180 degrees
- [ ] 360 degrees
- [ ] 90 degrees
- [ ] 270 degrees

2. The Pythagorean theorem applies to which type of triangle?

- [x] Right triangles
- [ ] Equilateral triangles
- [ ] Isosceles triangles
- [ ] All triangles

## Lesson 2.2: Circles

**Objectives:**

- Calculate circumference and area of circles
- Understand the relationship between radius and diameter

A circle is defined as all points equidistant from a center point.

# Module 3: Trigonometry

This module introduces trigonometric functions and their applications.

Estimated time: 10 hours
Prerequisites: Module 2 (Geometry)

## Lesson 3.1: Sine, Cosine, Tangent

**Objectives:**

- Define sine, cosine, and tangent
- Calculate trigonometric ratios for common angles

Trigonometry studies relationships between angles and sides of triangles.

### Activity: Exercise

Calculate sin(30°), cos(45°), and tan(60°).

### Quiz: Trig Basics Quiz

1. What is sin(90°)?

- [x] 1
- [ ] 0
- [ ] 0.5
- [ ] -1

2. What is the range of cosine function?

- [x] [-1, 1]
- [ ] [0, 1]
- [ ] [-∞, ∞]
- [ ] [0, ∞]

## Lesson 3.2: Trigonometric Identities

**Objectives:**

- Understand the Pythagorean identity
- Apply double-angle formulas

Trigonometric identities are equations involving trigonometric functions that are true for all values.

**Glossary:**

- sin²θ + cos²θ = 1: The fundamental Pythagorean identity
- Hypotenuse: The longest side of a right triangle, opposite the right angle
